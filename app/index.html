How this guide was built

Names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags are the official PlayStation 5 list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden until earned). Every guide below is written in our own words from those official descriptions, and each one names its sources. Where a place or step is not pinned down by the official description, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing it. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere in this app, and it makes no network requests at all — it works with the network off. Demo mode: opening the app with ?demo=1 seeds 13 earned trophies so the tracker can be previewed; your own saved progress is never touched by it.
